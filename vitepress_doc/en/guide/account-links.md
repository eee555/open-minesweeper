# Account Links

Account links connect your identity on different platforms to your Open Minesweeper account. You can use this feature to help other players recognize you across communities. The feature is available in the **Account Links** tab on your profile page.

You need to sign in before you can manage your own account links.

Profile refresh and replay synchronization are disabled while Saolei.wang migrates. Account linking, saved profiles and imported replays remain available. Use Manage to view saved import results.

## Linking Process

GitHub accounts are linked automatically through authorization, and Mineracer accounts after confirmation on the external site. Discord, Tencent QQ and other platforms require you to enter an ID and have a moderator verify it.

### GitHub

Select GitHub on your own Account Links page and click **Link with GitHub**. Confirm the GitHub account and authorize; you will return to OpenMS automatically. Moderator approval is unnecessary. This feature does not provide GitHub sign-in.

Your stable numeric GitHub ID is public. Click **Load / refresh GitHub profile** to view your current username and profile link. Your browser retrieves these details directly from GitHub; a failed request does not affect your account link. Changing your GitHub username does not change the linked identity.

Each OpenMS account can link one GitHub account, and each GitHub account can belong to one OpenMS account. You may unlink and authorize again. Unlinking on OpenMS does not revoke the application authorization in GitHub settings.

### Discord and Tencent QQ

Click the plus button on your Account Links page, select the platform and enter your numeric ID. Submit the request and contact a moderator for verification. Both are private links, visible only to you and site moderators. Contact a moderator to correct an ID or remove a link.

For Discord, enable Developer Mode under **User Settings → Advanced**, then right-click your avatar and choose **Copy User ID**. Enter the numeric User ID, rather than a username or display name. Linking Discord does not provide site login.

### Mineracer

1. In the **Account Links** tab on your own profile, find the Mineracer section and click **Generate link**. You do not need to enter a Mineracer user ID.
2. Click **Open link**, sign in to the Mineracer account you want to link in the new window, and confirm the link. Check that you are signed in to the correct account before confirming.
3. Switch back to the Open Minesweeper account links page manually. Mineracer does not redirect you back. This page checks for confirmation automatically, or you can click **Refresh status**.
4. Once linked, the page shows your Mineracer user ID and the binding update time. No moderator approval is needed.

The link is valid for 10 minutes, and the page shows the remaining time. If it expires, click **Generate new link**, open it, and confirm again. To stop the process, leave the request unconfirmed and let the link expire.

::: warning Check your account before confirming
Each Open Minesweeper account can link to only one Mineracer account, and each Mineracer account can link to only one Open Minesweeper account. Mineracer does not currently support unlinking, so Open Minesweeper cannot remove or replace a Mineracer account link.
:::

### Other platforms

1. On the account links page, if you still have other platforms available to link, you will see a plus button. Click it to open a dialog that guides you through the linking process.
2. After entering the platform and id, you need a moderator to verify the account so that nobody can claim someone else's account.

### About ids

The word "id" can be ambiguous. On some websites it may be confused with a username or nickname. For platforms that require manual ID entry, the id should be a string of digits. After you choose a platform, the page should show instructions for finding that platform's id. Mineracer user IDs are obtained automatically during authorization, so these entry requirements do not apply to Mineracer.

For platforms that require manual ID entry, enter the id carefully. Contact a moderator to correct a wrong id.

### Let a moderator know you
The moderators can see your link request. If they already know you, they will approve it directly. If they do not know you, you need to contact them for approval.

Saolei.wang, Minesweeper.Online, Bilibili, Tencent QQ, and Discord all support private messages. You can directly message a moderator on those platforms and tell them your Open Minesweeper id. If the information matches, the moderator should verify the account.

Authoritative Minesweeper lets you link your Saolei.wang and Minesweeper.Online accounts. If those accounts match the accounts linked on Open Minesweeper, a moderator should verify your Authoritative Minesweeper account.

## Privacy

Public links may let users on other platforms connect your account with your real-name identity on this site. Discord and Tencent QQ are private platforms: link summaries, IDs and profile details are visible only to you and site moderators.

## FAQ

### Why does it say "account not linked"?

For platforms that require manual ID entry, account links are reviewed by moderators to prevent account impersonation. You need to contact a moderator, usually through the group chat. If the moderator knows you and can confirm that you own the account, they will approve it directly. If they cannot confirm your identity, you also need to contact the moderator's account on the corresponding platform. You can find that information in the moderator's **Account Links** tab. Mineracer does not require moderator approval; the account is linked automatically after you confirm on Mineracer.

### Why does Mineracer keep showing "Waiting for Mineracer"?

Make sure you have opened the generated link and confirmed on Mineracer, then return to Open Minesweeper and wait for the status to update, or click **Refresh status**. Generating or opening the link alone does not complete the process. If the link has expired, generate a new one.

### What if my Mineracer account is already linked to another user?

A Mineracer account can link to only one Open Minesweeper account. Check which account you are signed in to on both platforms. You cannot link the same Mineracer account to a second Open Minesweeper account, and transferring a link by unlinking is not currently supported.

### I entered the wrong id. What should I do?

The website currently does not support direct id editing. Please contact a moderator.

### Why are creator platforms not private?

We plan to develop a creator feature that lets you promote your minesweeper contents on Open Minesweeper. Once you start promoting your work, your account on that creator platform is already public. If you do not need to promote your work, there is no need to link a creator account, and we do not recommend doing so.

### How do I remove an account link?

Mineracer links cannot currently be removed or replaced, including by moderators through the site's account linking tools. For other platforms, contact a moderator.
