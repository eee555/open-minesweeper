# Memorial Accounts

A memorial account preserves the record of a legendary player we cannot currently reach. **"Memorial" does not mean the player has passed away**, nor that the account is banned or hidden. The player can contact us at any time to reclaim ownership of the account.

## How to recognize one

A **Memorial account** badge appears next to the player's name on their profile and in rankings. Their profile, results and replays remain publicly visible.

## Results and rankings

Replays, results and automatic medals of a memorial account count towards the rankings just as they do for any other account. Ranking and medal calculations do not exclude these accounts, and existing account links take part as usual.

## Maintained by the site

A memorial account cannot sign in or recover its password by email. Its profile details, replay uploads and account links are therefore maintained by a superuser or moderator on its behalf and cannot be managed by the player directly. Only a superuser can assign links for Saolei.wang, MSGames and Minesweeper.Online. Contact the superuser if a historical replay needs to be added or a detail corrected. For regular accounts, see [Account Links](./account-links.md).

## FAQ

### Can I turn my own account into a memorial account?
No. Memorial accounts are created and maintained by the superuser, with no self-service setup.

### How can the player reclaim the account?
Contact the superuser, who will help you regain ownership. There is no self-service reclaim process on the site.

### Why is the badge missing on some pages?
Names and badges are cached. Other browsers may temporarily show an old name or no badge until the normal refresh catches up.

### Can a memorial account still upload replays?
Not by the player, since the account cannot sign in. A superuser can add historical replays, and those replays count towards results and rankings as usual.
