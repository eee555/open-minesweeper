# Weekly Tournament

Open Minesweeper hosts Speed Weekly and Efficiency Weekly tournaments independently. You may enter both in the same week; register separately and use each tournament's own [token](./identifier.md#tournament-token).

Registration requires a real name or verification by a verified player. See [Minesweeper Tournaments](./tournament.md).

## Rules

**Speed Weekly and Efficiency Weekly each have an independent two-hour session. You may choose separate times within the same week, and the sessions may overlap; you do not have to enter both at the same time.**

The tournament starts at 00:00 UTC on Monday and ends at 24:00 UTC on Sunday. You may choose a two-hour window separately for each tournament within that interval. During the tournament, you can register on its page and receive a unique tournament identifier. After registration, you have two hours to upload tournament replays. If you register after 22:00 UTC on Sunday, your tournament end time is still 24:00 UTC on Sunday, so please pay attention to the time.

After confirmation, your two-hour session starts immediately and cannot be cancelled or chosen again by yourself. If you registered by mistake and have no tournament replays, contact the host or an administrator after your personal window expires to reset it. The overall tournament must still be running.

Both tournaments count only official Standard replays, including NF. The software must support tournament tokens.

### Speed Weekly

The classic 2E+5I format adds the fastest 2 Expert and 5 Intermediate times. Intermediate times must be below 60 seconds and Expert times below 240 seconds. Missing games count as these respective limits.

### Efficiency Weekly

Each game is scored by `Time / IOE²` (time divided by the square of IOE); lower is better. Add the lowest scores from 2 Expert and 5 Intermediate games. Intermediate scores must be below 60, Expert below 240, with no additional IOE cap. Missing games count as 60 and 240 respectively.

## Settlement and Disqualification

Final results are calculated no earlier than one hour after the overall tournament ends. This does not extend your session or upload window. After awards, only the tournament host may disqualify a player for breaking the rules. Awarded points are revoked; replay files are kept.

## Point Rewards

After the tournament ends, points are awarded based on rank. The formula is `50/rank`: for example, 1st place receives 50 points, 2nd place receives 25 points, and so on.
